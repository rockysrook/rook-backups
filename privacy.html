Privacy policy

Rook Backups is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to upload, list and delete the owner's own encrypted backup files.

  • It collects no personal information from anyone.
  • It shares no data with anyone and sends nothing to any third party.
  • Google Drive data is used only for storing those backups and is not used for advertising, analytics or AI training.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: contact the owner of the Google account that registered this app.
